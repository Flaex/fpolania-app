## Introduction: Much More Than "Letters"
Typography is another name for the letters of the alphabet, but its real weight is incalculable. It is one of the fundamental elements not only in visual communication but also in the development of human civilization.

## 1. Etymology and Historical Significance
The term has its origin in the Greek *Typos* (impression) and *Graphia* (writing). Historically, it refers to **"printed writing"**. Over the centuries, it has evolved from the cuneiform inscriptions of the **Sumerians** to the invention of the printing press by **Gutenberg** and movable type.
What we call typography today also refers to the style, order, and appearance of the letters, numbers, and symbols created in the communication process.

## 2. Typography as Brand Voice
In my product architecture practice, typography is the voice of the business. If the typography is inconsistent or difficult to read, the message loses authority. From the Book of Kells to modern interfaces, typography has been the tool for recording and democratizing knowledge.
