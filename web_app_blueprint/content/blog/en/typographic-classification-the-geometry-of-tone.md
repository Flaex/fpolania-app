# TYPOGRAPHIC CLASSIFICATION: THE GEOMETRY OF TONE (High-Fidelity Overhaul)
**Pillar:** Integrated Design (The Visual Lens)
**Original Language:** Spanish

## The Technique of Intentional Selection
Typography is defined as the art and technique of selecting font families, sizes, lengths, and line spacings. It is not a decorative choice; it is a decision of **information architecture**.

## 1. The Vox-ATypI System: Categories of Authority
To build coherent design systems, I use the technical classification to determine the brand's voice:
*   **Venetians (1400-1500):** Related to manual writing, they convey tradition and warmth (low contrast).
*   **Garalds (1600):** Baroque influence, fluid forms, and medium contrast. Classic elegance.
*   **Transitional (1700):** Vertical axes and marked contrast (e.g., Baskerville). They convey sophistication and order.
*   **Didones (1700-1800):** Extreme contrast and vertical modulation (e.g., Bodoni). They convey luxury and technical precision.
*   **Mechanistic (Slab Serif):** Robust square serifs, born for industrial advertising strength.
*   **Sans Serif:** Simplicity, modernity, and maximum digital legibility. The contemporary standard.
*   **Modern (1900-today):** Includes glyphic, calligraphic, and experimental types.

## 2. Conclusion: Architect's Decisions
Each typographic family has a different psychological charge. My role is to ensure that the choice for your project is not a visual preference but a **strategic decision** that reinforces the authority of your message.
